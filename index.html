AI Phone Interpreter

● Live MVP

A bidirectional, real-time voice interpreter between Telugu and English. You speak in one language, and the other person hears you in theirs. The current MVP connects a laptop and an Android phone over WebSockets. The goal is an app where two phones in different places hold a normal call with live translation in between.

Streaming, both ways

FastAPI + WebSockets carry audio in both directions, so either side can start talking.

Next languages

Hindi and Tamil, once Telugu–English is tested end to end.

Campus Navigation Assistant

In development · IIT Hyderabad pilot

An AI navigation and object-detection system for visually impaired people. A phone app guides the user along a walking route and warns about obstacles in the way, using a YOLO detection model running behind a FastAPI backend. The MVP covers two real campus routes, from a hostel to the Main Mess and from a hostel to Hostel Circle.

Phone app + AI backend

Flutter Android app streams the camera view to a Python FastAPI server for detection and guidance.

Own dataset, in progress

Recording walking videos around campus to label what stock YOLO misses: poles, steps and kerbs.

Scoped on purpose

Two real routes first. Make them reliable before adding more of the campus.
